Study builder

Describe a study and build it. Choose a phase and size, or open a CDISC protocol and the study follows its schedule of activities; say what an efficacy score should do by arm; choose the layers and raw domains. The first build starts R in this browser, which takes about half a minute.

When it is built, the study opens here in the study view, where you can save it. Saved studies and settings are listed in the portfolio.

Everything runs in this browser. Nothing is sent anywhere. Data is simulated by gsm.datasim.
