Study builder

Describe a study and build it. Choose a phase and size, or open a CDISC protocol and the study follows its schedule of activities; say what an efficacy score should do by arm; choose the layers and raw domains. The first build starts R in this browser, which takes about a minute.

When it is built, the study opens here in the study viewer, where you can save it. Saved studies and settings are listed in the study list.

Everything runs in this browser, and no study data is sent anywhere; building a study downloads R and its packages from webr.r-wasm.org and repo.r-wasm.org. Data is simulated by a modified copy of gsm.datasim 2.1.0: the additions are not Gilead's. Example protocols: content based on DDF-RA (GitHub), used under the CC-BY-4.0 licence, read and cut down here.
