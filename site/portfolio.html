Portfolio

Every study you can have, in one table. A study with its data ready opens at once; one that is settings only is built first. Studies and settings you save stay in this browser.

Open takes you to the study view. Build, Edit and New study take you to the study builder.

Everything runs in this browser. Nothing is sent anywhere. Data is simulated by gsm.datasim.
