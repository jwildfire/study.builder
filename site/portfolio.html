Portfolio

Every study you can have, in one table. A study with its data ready opens at once; one that is settings only is built first. Studies and settings you save stay in this browser. The protocols a new study can follow are below.

Open takes you to the study view. Build, Edit and New study take you to the study builder.

Protocols

A study can follow a protocol's schedule of activities. study.builder reads a CDISC USDM study definition, version 4: the data form of an ICH M11 protocol. These are CDISC's own five public examples, each as the reader makes it, and each builds a study with real R.

Open one in the study builder to see its schedule as a grid, change it, and build. Your own USDM file opens there too.

Fetching the protocols…

Everything runs in this browser. Nothing is sent anywhere. Data is simulated by gsm.datasim.
