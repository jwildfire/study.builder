Portfolio

Every study you can have, in one table, a row a study's configuration. Any row opens in the study builder. One with its data ready also opens as a study, at once. A protocol a new study can follow is a row too: CDISC's five public USDM examples, the data form of an ICH M11 protocol. Studies and settings you save stay in this browser.

Overview takes you to the study view. Open in builder and New study take you to the study builder.

Everything runs in this browser, and no study data is sent anywhere; building a study downloads R and its packages from webr.r-wasm.org and repo.r-wasm.org. Data is simulated by a modified copy of gsm.datasim 2.1.0: the additions are not Gilead's. Example protocols: content based on DDF-RA (GitHub), used under the CC-BY-4.0 licence, read and cut down here.
