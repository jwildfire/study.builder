Study view

One study, looked through in five sections. Overview counts it. Design has the protocol it follows and its schedule. Data has the pipeline, every table and every column. Operations has the site metrics, the adverse events with no arm named, and one participant at a time. Reporting has the results by arm. A study that kept its monthly snapshots has a Snapshot picker.

Other studies are in the portfolio. Make your own in the study builder.

Everything runs in this browser. Nothing is sent anywhere. Data is simulated by gsm.datasim.
