Study view

One study, looked through. Overview counts it; Schedule and Design show the protocol it follows; Efficacy shows a score by arm; Tables, Columns and One participant go through every layer. A study that kept its monthly snapshots has a Snapshot picker.

Other studies are in the portfolio. Make your own in the study builder.

Everything runs in this browser. Nothing is sent anywhere. Data is simulated by gsm.datasim.
