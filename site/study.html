Study viewer

One study, looked through in five sections. Overview counts it. Design has the protocol it follows and its schedule. Data has the pipeline, every table and every column. Operations has the site metrics, the adverse events with no arm named, and one participant at a time. Reporting has the results by arm. A study that kept its monthly snapshots has a Snapshot picker.

Other studies are in the study list. Make your own in the study builder.

Everything runs in this browser, and no study data is sent anywhere; building a study downloads R and its packages from webr.r-wasm.org and repo.r-wasm.org. Data is simulated by a modified copy of gsm.datasim 2.1.0: the additions are not Gilead's. Example protocols: content based on DDF-RA (GitHub), used under the CC-BY-4.0 licence, read and cut down here.
